# ehpa — Effinger Health PA: William's standing billing rules

- Medicare first, Medicaid / LTC second. Hospice patients: Medicare with modifier GV. A 9-digit AARP number is UHC Medicare Advantage (UNI20).
- PRIMARY claims more than 12 months from the date of service and unpaid are written off, any carrier — "no chance of it being resubmitted for actual payment" (9/7/2026).
- A SECONDARY / supplement balance is timely from the PRIMARY'S remit date, never the date of service — rebill it, never age it out (9/7/2026).
- Wrong-payer denials never stay on the patient balance. Medicaid crossover paying $0 on Medicare coinsurance = contractual write-off.
- UHC (UNI20) and Florida Blue (BCBS) visits older than 5/1/2026 are WORKED, no longer frozen (William 10/6/2026): first look for an answer already received (ERA / Availity / UHC portal export / a $0 posting) and post or work it; otherwise send ONCE (frequency 1, or 7 with the ICN when the payer already adjudicated) and add it to registers/tf_writeoff_watch.json; a timely-filing answer -> WOLATE write-off. Never send a claim older than 12 months to Medicare.
- Frequency 1 (original claim) before every rebill; frequency 7 only with the payer's ICN from William.
- No group numbers on any insurance plan. No SSNs anywhere.
- Address placeholder when a chart has no street address: line 1 = HCA (hospital), SNF (nursing home), ALF (ALF / home visit); zip 32308-4486; use the pop-up's Edit address.
- Insurance lookup order: patient database -> AMD eligibility (free 271) -> Stedi -> facesheet -> William. If AMD eligibility is down, skip to Stedi.
- Florida Medicaid's 271 names the SMMC plan; a patient in "SMMC MMA Capitated — FLORIDA COMMUNITY CARE" (FLO99) or Humana / Sunshine / Simply MMA is billed to that plan, not Medicaid fee-for-service.
- One AMD login attempt per run. Every call learned from a DevTools paste is written to docs/captured_calls.md the same night; not in the registry = not captured, ask instead of guessing.
- Protected health information travels only to William (SendUserFile, the boards, Gmail to himself) — never into this repository, a skill, or a public page.

Caps: 150 checks or $25 a run, 400 checks or $100 a month. Only the nightly cascade routine asks, on Sundays, with `claim_status_sweep.py --go`; without --go a claim-status helper asks nothing. The Sunday step first runs aging_refresh.py as ONE Bash call with the longest timeout (it takes about 3 minutes; cut off at the 2-minute default it writes no ageing file and the sweep then asks nothing).
- (11/10/2026) A claim-status answer is FINAL (paid, denied, finalized at $0) only when it names the claim (the account number the payer echoes = the visit id + claim letter, or the payer's claim number) or was asked with the exact date of service. A paid, denied or $0 answer from the 3-day window that does not name the claim, an answer whose claims all carry other visits' account numbers, and a 'not matched' answer get ONE check with the exact date first; an exact-date answer holding several claims, none of them ours, is 'not matched' and goes on the hand-off list - it is never recorded as our final answer.
- (11/10/2026) A claim-status answer 'not eligible on the date of service' (codes 88 / 97 outside a final denial) is a coverage problem: it goes to the insurance check (the STEP 1 cascade), never to a resend. Only 'claim not found' (code 35) stays a resend candidate. 'Cannot provide further status electronically' (code 0) and a 'not matched' that the exact-date check does not cure go on the hand-off list: ask the payer by portal or phone.
- (11/10/2026) HAND-OFF LIST: two answers in a row with no usable content, or three 'pending' answers in a row, end the electronic asking; an empty answer counts toward the two only when another claim of the same payer answered that night, and four answers in a row with no usable content end the asking either way (a claim that always comes back empty, or a payer that answers nobody, is no longer bought every week). The claim is listed with its age and dollars in every Sunday report while it is still open (nothing posted for its payer) and under a year old, until it is sent again or a person re-opens it in the repository's ledger (`python3 cs_rules.py --clear <visit id> --ledger <clone>/practices/ehpa/registers/claim_status_ledger.json`, then commit). A payer with claims on the list is named in a 'PROPOSE FOR CS_OFF' line only on evidence about the payer itself: one of those claims was handed off after four answers in a row with no usable content, and none of that payer's claims has had a usable answer from 7 days before the first of those four until now - the whole span of the four tries, the Sunday before them in weekly runs, and since (third fix round, 11/10/2026). So a payer that answered any of its claims, even 'pending' or paid, the Sunday before the first try or on any night since is never named (the claim that always comes back empty is handed off all the same). Only a person switches a payer off.
- (11/10/2026) A claim with a FINAL answer on file and still nothing posted for its payer is listed in every Sunday report ('FINAL ANSWER ON FILE BUT NOTHING POSTED FOR ITS PAYER') - no new check is bought for it; a 'paid' one is an unposted remit.
- (11/10/2026) Claim status is never asked of the military plan (TRI00, TRI05) or the Medigap plan AAR01 - every check came back empty; those claims are listed for portal or phone.
- (11/10/2026) The Sunday claim-status run writes claimstatus_<date>.json and the ledger after EVERY claim: a run the Bash tool cuts off keeps every answer where the runbook reads it; it is not started again that night.
