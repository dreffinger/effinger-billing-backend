# effinger-billing-backend

## Brand

The ehpa main logo and colour scheme are the design of the ehpa Billing Button page, saved in `brand/`
(William, 2026-10-12). Every new page, app, installer or document for ehpa uses `brand/ehpa-logo.svg` (or `ehpa-mark.svg`
on dark backgrounds) and the Midnight tokens in `brand/ehpa-tokens.css`: dark first, Quicksand + JetBrains Mono, the
sky-to-magenta gradient on the primary action. Read `brand/README.md` before designing anything. The ring of the mark opens
at the right, running down; never rotate or recolour it.
