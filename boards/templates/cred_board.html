Vendors (looked at 9/6/2026)

Verdict: build on primary sources; no vendor for now. Verifiable, Medallion and Assured are enterprise, sales-led products — no self-serve signup, no published pricing, no trial. Their APIs are real but open only to contracted customers (buyer data: Verifiable median ~$220K/yr, Medallion ~$67K/yr). Verifiable's CredAgent (Feb 2026) automates primary-source verification, not payer enrollment. Only Medallion and Assured actually file payer applications for you. Assured says it is heading for independent physicians — re-check March 2027, or sooner if the practice adds providers.

How reminders work

Every card with a due date gets reminders at set lead times: 120-day items (CAQH) at 14 and 3 days; licenses, DEA, board and malpractice at 90, 30 and 7 days; Medicare and Medicaid revalidation the week a due date appears on the CMS list; payer recredentialing 36 months from last approval once known. Each reminder lands in chat and flips the card to your turn with what to do and where to log in. Post the renewal confirmation on the card (or set the new date below the card) and the reminders clear themselves.

Glossary

NPI
National Provider Identifier — 10 digits, one per provider (type 1) and one for the group (type 2). Lives in NPPES; must be updated within 30 days of any change.
NPPES
The federal NPI registry. Payers pull address and taxonomy from it, so a stale record causes rejections.
PECOS
Medicare's enrollment system. Revalidation every 5 years; missing it deactivates billing with no retroactive pay.
PTAN
Medicare's provider transaction number — one per provider per group enrollment. Ours: group IZ039A, Effinger FV002T, Garcia KE642.
Revalidation
Medicare's and Medicaid's periodic re-enrollment (5-year cycle). Different from payer recredentialing.
Recredentialing
Commercial and Medicaid managed-care plans re-verify credentials every 36 months (NCQA standard), counted from the last approval.
CAQH
The shared credentialing profile most commercial payers read (now "CAQH Provider Data Portal"). Must be re-attested every 120 days or payers treat it as stale.
LEIE
OIG's List of Excluded Individuals/Entities. Billing with an excluded person on staff is a false-claims problem, so payers expect monthly checks.
MQA
Florida Department of Health's Medical Quality Assurance — the licensing authority for the DO and APRN licenses.
HETS / HEET
Medicare's eligibility system and First Coast's attestation tool for it; done 9/5/2026 via AdvancedMD's submitter VQAS.
ERA / EFT
Electronic remittance and payment. Each payer needs its own enrollment; the payer cards say which are actually posting into AdvancedMD.
Primary-source verification
Checking a credential with the body that issued it (state board, DEA, NPDB, board of certification) rather than the provider's copy.
