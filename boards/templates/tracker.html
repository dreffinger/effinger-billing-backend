Insurance Refresh Board

Patients whose claims are waiting on a plan, an MBI, or a decision. Each card starts top-left with the facility the card's charge came from and where the patient lives (ALF resident / SNF resident; hospital stays show the hospital and the home facility underneath; a plain SNF / ALF only when no facility is on file), then leads with the one thing needed now. Everything else — the rest of the need, the lookup trail, the API results and every note — sits behind the Details fold on the card; a card you open stays open while you work. New = Claude added something since your last note on that row. The floating button (bottom right) opens a billing chat that can log into AMD and work the board live with you.
