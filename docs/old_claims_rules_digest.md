# Rules digest for the old-claims disposition review (Effinger Health PA, 10/9/2026)

Standing rules William gave (practices/ehpa/rules.md, docs/SHARED_LESSONS.md, the billing skills):

1. PRIMARY claims more than 12 months from the date of service and still unpaid are written off - any carrier (9/7/2026). A SECONDARY / supplement balance is timely from the PRIMARY'S remit date, never from the DOS: it is rebilled, never aged out (9/7). Never send a claim older than 12 months to Medicare.
2. Write-off codes: WOINS = untimely / never-adjudicated primary over 12 months (dated note) or a contractual remainder; WOLATE = the payer answered untimely / not on file / denied and the claim is past filing; WOPDEOB = "client paid per EOB, EOB not forwarded" (payer says paid, remit never received); WOCAID2ND = dual patient, Medicaid secondary residual after Medicare/MA paid; WOMCAD = residual on a Medicaid-primary charge; WONCS = non-covered service; WOSMBAL = small balance under $25 per patient. Never WOCOUR across the board on Medicare/MA cost-sharing.
3. Adjudicated cost-sharing (a PR deductible/coinsurance left after a payer payment) on a non-Medicaid patient is NOT a write-off - it goes to the supplement or a patient statement; on a QMB/dual it is written off with the Medicaid code. A visit that already carries a non-void payment (even $0 with reason codes) has been adjudicated - never rebill a paid visit, never write off its remainder as "untimely".
4. A $0 posting with CAS/denial reason codes is the payer's answer (a denial), not an unpaid claim: inside 12 months it goes to the denial cascade (diagnosis / eligibility / payer fixes), never a write-off by itself.
5. Frequency 1 (original claim) before every rebill; ONE rebill per visit; a claim the payer already adjudicated is never re-sent as frequency 1 (that draws an OA-18 duplicate) - a correction goes as frequency 7 with the payer's claim number. A visit whose last bill date is within 30 days is not resent.
6. UNI20 (UnitedHealthcare) and BCBS (Florida Blue) visits before 5/1/2026 are WORKED, not frozen (10/6/2026): an answer already received (ERA, Availity, a $0 posting, a 277) is posted / worked; otherwise the visit is sent ONCE and added to tf_writeoff_watch.json; a payer timely-filing answer (CARC 29 on the payer's own 835/Availity line) -> WOLATE. A visit on the watch is never resent.
7. The Stedi 277 (claim status) for a UHC claim re-sent as frequency 1 lists the ORIGINAL 2025 payment (check number, paid date, payer claim number) above the F2 585 duplicate denials - that original payment is real money UHC paid that AMD never posted (no 835 reached AMD; the UNI20 ERA feed was broken). A 277 D0 "claim not found" on a visit that already carries a payer ICN is a search miss, not a never-received claim.
8. AET12 (Aetna Senior Supplemental, car42009) is ON HOLD (9/21): crossover claims, never rebilled through Change Healthcare; worked from the Aetna side.
9. DECEASED: a charge with a date of service AFTER the date of death is VOIDED (10/4), never written off and never sent; the deceased guard blocks rebills. A chart with a deceased flag needs the DOS checked against the date of death before any action.
10. Hospice: a CO-B9 hospice denial on an E&M visit = add modifier GV and rebill (9/20); on a CCM claim (99487/99489) = WONCS write-off (10/4). CCM is a monthly claim: a CO-151 frequency denial = re-date to the last day of the month and send as original; two CCM claims in one month on one chart = void the duplicate.
11. Medicaid-plan residuals: Florida Community Care (FLO99/FCC) $0 statements decode 041 = primary paid >= Medicaid allowed -> WOCAID2ND; 14/075 = no fee-schedule line (CCM/AWV/ACP never payable) -> WOCAID2ND secondary / WONCS primary. Humana Medicaid residual CO185 on Medicare-paid lines -> WOINS contractual.
12. manual_post (addpayments) carries NO reason codes: a secondary claim behind a hand-posted primary payment stops in Unbilled ("Payment reason codes are missing or do not balance"). For an old claim closed from a 277 that is accepted and listed; for a visit with a live supplement secondary the remainder is left open and flagged.
13. Never bill a VA CCN visit without the VA authorization number; TRICARE East claims reject for Pam Garcia (CO185, not credentialed) - a credentialing item, not a chart write-off inside 12 months.
14. Wrong-payer denials never stay on the patient balance; a balance a $0 posting moved to the patient side is moved back to insurance (move_to_ins) before any insurance write-off.

The agreed shape: (1) 277 says paid WITH amount + check number -> post that payment with that check number and date, remainder contractual (never an invented amount); (2) payer says paid/finalized but gives no amount -> WOPDEOB with a dated note; (3) primary over 12 months never adjudicated -> write off (WOINS / WOLATE); (4) secondaries and supplements -> skipped (checked against the primary's remit date first).
