# Medicaid credentialing kit for a practice (Florida)

Two lists to send a practice we are credentialing: **List A** is what they send us; **List B** is what only they can do.
Everything else (filling the applications, pre-filling every form, tracking status, chasing deficiencies) is ours.
Replace the bracketed parts. For another state keep the shape and swap the state items (see `README.md`).

---

## List A - send us these documents

Send each as a clear PDF or phone photo (all four corners, readable). Upload them where we tell you or email them to
yourself with the subject "credentialing documents". Identity documents go into a locked folder only you and we can open.

### For the practice (the group)
- [ ] IRS letter showing the legal name and EIN (CP-575 or 147C) and a signed W-9
- [ ] Articles of incorporation / Sunbiz annual report (shows the officers and owners)
- [ ] Every owner, officer and managing employee with 5% or more ownership or control: full name, title, ownership %
      (their SSN, date of birth and home address go on the application; we collect them through the locked folder)
- [ ] Voided check or bank letter for EFT
- [ ] Practice addresses: service location, mailing, pay-to (and old addresses to remove)
- [ ] Group NPI and current Medicaid / Medicare IDs if any, with the latest letters from Medicaid (renewal notices,
      approval letters, deficiency letters)
- [ ] Malpractice certificate of insurance for the group (if separate) and any local business tax receipt / permits

### For each clinician
- [ ] State license (we print the verification ourselves; send the license number)
- [ ] DEA registration certificate (number and expiration)
- [ ] Board or national certification certificate (APRN: AANP / ANCC)
- [ ] Malpractice certificate of insurance (carrier, policy number, dates, limits)
- [ ] CV with month/year dates and an explanation of any gap of 6 months or more
- [ ] Driver's license and Social Security card **in the current legal name** (Medicaid rejects a card in a prior name;
      if the name changed, the marriage certificate or court order too)
- [ ] NPI, CAQH ID, Medicare PTAN, any prior Medicaid ID
- [ ] APRN or PA: the name, license number and Medicaid ID of the collaborating or supervising physician
- [ ] Any license discipline, criminal history, payment suspension, or prior termination: the final order or settlement,
      monitoring program contract and completion letter, and a short signed explanation in their own words
- [ ] Fingerprint (Level 2) screening: the Clearinghouse result page if they have one, or tell us they need one

## List B - things only you can do

Each item takes minutes; we send the exact link and click path when it is time.

### Practice owner
1. **Create or recover the Medicaid secure portal login** (the PIN letter comes by mail after enrollment) and keep the
   password in your password manager.
2. **Sign** the Medicaid Provider Agreement (we pre-fill it; the name and title must match the Owners panel exactly) and
   any payer agreements.
3. **Clearinghouse:** request your provider access (sign the user agreement we pre-fill) and start or renew each
   person's fingerprint screening.
4. **NPPES:** fix the practice's NPI record when we flag something (addresses, authorized official).
5. **Bank:** confirm the EFT account and approve the test deposit.
6. Tell us when any address, owner, bank account or clinician changes (Medicaid needs it within 30 days).

### Each clinician
1. **Sign** your Medicaid Provider Agreement page and, for an APRN or PA, the collaboration form (both of you sign).
2. **Fingerprints:** go to the Livescan appointment if the Clearinghouse has no current prints.
3. **CAQH:** re-attest every 120 days (we remind you).
4. **NPPES:** update your own NPI record (taxonomy, license number with the current prefix, practice address).
5. **Renew** your license and DEA on time and send us the new certificates.

## What we do (so nobody duplicates it)
- Fill every application and form, compare names and numbers across license, NPPES, IRS and Medicaid records before
  anything is filed (a single typo travels from one file to another).
- Track every application with the public status page and fix deficiencies the same week.
- Keep each document in the practice's Credentialing Vault and the expiry calendar on the credentialing board.
